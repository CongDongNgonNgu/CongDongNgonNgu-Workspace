## Design-only smaller alternative

Read-only review of current `src/attachments/video-processing/video-worker.ts` shows **remux**, not H264/AAC encoding: fully decode inputs/outputs, stream-copy selected H264/AAC MP4 tracks while removing SEI with `filter_units`, produce one scaled MJPEG thumbnail, then fully decode that JPEG.

A small source-built separate CLI pair at the same exact FFmpeg commit appears technically feasible on existing Linux CI, with no paid infrastructure: start from disabled components, disable autodetect/network/hardware/external libraries/GPL/nonfree, and explicitly enable only required internal components. Candidate capabilities: H264/AAC/MJPEG decoders and required parsers; MOV/MP4 and JPEG image demuxing; MP4/MOV, image2 and null muxing; file/pipe protocols; `filter_units` plus its internal coded-bitstream dependencies; scale/format/buffer/sink and necessary audio format/resample filters; avcodec/avformat/avfilter/swscale/swresample. Existing null-output full-decode commands also require their selected output encoders (normally wrapped_avframe and PCM_s16le); MJPEG encoding is required for the thumbnail. A build that only enables H264/AAC decoders and MJPEG would be incomplete. No H264/AAC encoder, x264, x265 or external FFT library is required by current production worker arguments. CI fixture generation may have additional encoder requirements and must be audited separately rather than broadening the deployed binary automatically.

These are candidate requirements, **not a frozen configure command or successful build**. Exact dependency selection and component availability need source review and positive/negative fixtures. Current worker/version/archive allowlists, manifest verification and CI must be amended under independent review; no unchecked fallback. Pin source archive and toolchain provenance, retain complete source/licenses/build recipe with the binary, verify resulting component inventory and actual pipeline on CI/Render, and remeasure deadline/RSS/headroom.
