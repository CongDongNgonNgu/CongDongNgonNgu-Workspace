# Product Principles

1. Independent product: reuse technical foundations, not EduAI product identity or LMS assumptions.
2. Free creates real value: public/community learning, basic exchange and useful resources remain accessible.
3. People first, AI second: AI helps users prepare, understand and transform community knowledge; it does not replace human contribution.
4. Contribution compounds: corrections, translations, examples and cultural notes become structured reusable knowledge after review.
5. Trust is visible: resources expose provenance, review state and attribution.
6. Safety by design: block/report/privacy/contact permissions are core flows.
7. Mobile first-class: all core flows work from 320px upward.
8. Accessible by default: semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus, contrast and reduced-motion support.
9. No addictive manipulation: avoid casino-style points, shame mechanics and engagement pressure.
10. No membership dark patterns: clear price, entitlements, renewal/cancel state and contribution-earned alternatives.
11. Extensible language domain: add languages through data/configuration rather than schema duplication.
12. “Vietnamese for the World” is first-class.
13. Evidence over assumption: architecture, UAT and production claims require reproducible evidence.
