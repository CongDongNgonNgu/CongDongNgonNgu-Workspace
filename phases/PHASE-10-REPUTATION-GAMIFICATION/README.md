# Phase 10 — Reputation, Learning XP & Healthy Gamification

## Goal
Reward learning consistency and valuable community contribution without turning the product into a casino or allowing easily farmed vanity metrics.

## Two independent systems
- **Learning XP:** practice activity, learning milestones and streak-related progress.
- **Community Reputation:** useful answers, corrections, translations, verification and other trusted contribution.

Both systems use auditable ledgers/events rather than a mutable total as the only source of truth.

## Completion gate
Ledger, rules engine, badges/streaks, contributor levels, reversal/anti-farming behavior and UI are implemented/tested; reputation does not grant privileged roles automatically; Stitch/responsive/a11y/CI evidence complete.
