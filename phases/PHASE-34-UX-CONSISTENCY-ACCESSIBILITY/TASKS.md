## LNG-34-001 — Cross-route inventory
Freeze critical route/state matrix across Explore, Community, Language Hub, partner exchange, Groups, Messages, profile/passport, progress and Library.

## LNG-34-002 — Design-system consistency
Normalize CTA hierarchy, icon components, form/control sizing, cards, typography/spacing and shared states without erasing intentional feature differences.

## LNG-34-003 — Responsive/footer/whitespace sweep
Remove unjustified large dead zones, overflow and awkward mobile/footer compositions at all seven widths.

## LNG-34-004 — Accessibility sweep
Keyboard/focus, semantic controls, accessible names, expanded/collapsed state, contrast, reduced-motion behavior where applicable and bounded automated audit.

## LNG-34-005 — Navigation/state sweep
Verify CTA routing, active state, direct URL, refresh, back/forward and route transitions across major surfaces.

## LNG-34-006 — Final UX integration acceptance
Independent review, ordinary remediation, exact-main deployed TEST proof, cleanup and lifecycle closure. Do not add new major features here.
