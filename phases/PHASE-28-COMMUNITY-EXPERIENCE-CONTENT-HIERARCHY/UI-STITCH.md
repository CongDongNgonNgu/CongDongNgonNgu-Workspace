## Required screens/states
- Community discovery desktop and mobile.
- Feed card variants for discussion/question/correction and action-capability differences.
- Detail page hierarchy for discussion/question/correction.
- Correction proposal + explanation + contextual replies.
- Community→Language discovery CTA.
- Sidebar/accordion treatments.
- empty/no-result/loading/error examples.

## Design principles
- Preserve existing CongDongNgonNgu navy/orange identity and typography language.
- Social interaction should feel active and inviting, not pale/admin-like.
- Actual user content outranks generic page labels.
- Learning metadata is useful but secondary.
- Do not copy Facebook/EduAI literally; borrow interaction clarity and energy only.
- Use canonical icons; no literal `v` arrow.
- Keep touch targets and mobile density comfortable.
