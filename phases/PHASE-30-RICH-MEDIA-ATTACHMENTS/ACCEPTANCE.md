# Phase 30 Acceptance

- One shared attachment contract is used across supported surfaces.
- Image/short-video/approved-file validation uses MIME and content-signature checks where applicable.
- Private attachments cannot be opened by unauthorized users or by possession of a UI reference alone.
- Size/count/rate limits and deletion/orphan cleanup are defined and tested.
- Upload progress/retry/remove states are clear and accessible.
- Unsupported/corrupt files fail safely.
- Community/Group/Message integrations preserve their existing authorization and semantic contracts.
- Full automated, security, responsive, cleanup and PR/main lifecycle gates pass.
