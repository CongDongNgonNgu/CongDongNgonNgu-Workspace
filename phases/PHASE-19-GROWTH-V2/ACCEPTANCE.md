# Phase 19 Acceptance

- Priorities are grounded in post-launch evidence, not competitor copying or novelty.
- Each selected V2 feature has a problem statement, target metric, security/privacy/licensing review, architecture decision and implementation/release plan.
- Speech/pronunciation claims are not shipped without validated provider/scoring evidence and consent policy.
- Common Voice/open data research uses current authoritative terms before ingestion.
- Marketplace work does not start before payout/refund/legal/moderation model is approved.
- Native app decision compares measured PWA gaps and avoids unnecessary duplicate business logic.
- ML/RAG has a simple baseline and evaluation plan; provenance/authorization remain mandatory.
- Deferred/rejected candidates do not leave broken placeholder UI.
