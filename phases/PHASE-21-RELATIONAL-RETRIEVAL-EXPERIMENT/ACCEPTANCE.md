# Phase 21 Acceptance

Prospective contract only.

- Lexical baseline and relational candidate use the same frozen eligible resource pool and reviewed query labels.
- Quality criteria are fixed before comparison; report denominators and uncertainty honestly.
- `precision@k`, `recall@k`, abstention/support behavior or any accepted replacement metrics are computed only from observed benchmark results.
- Every returned resource is currently eligible at response time and retains source/provenance/license context.
- No known authorization, provenance, redistribution or citation leak exists in accepted cases.
- Source invalidation/deletion removes or invalidates stale relations, projections, caches and citations as required.
- Lexical/source-card fallback remains available when relational support is absent or invalid.
- No generated answer, embedding provider, graph/vector service or production index is activated.

Close with `GO`, `DEFER`, or `REJECT`. `GO` means the bounded retrieval technique met the accepted technical/quality threshold; it does not prove real-user usefulness or adoption.
