# Phase 10 Acceptance

- Learning XP and Community Reputation are separate ledger-backed systems.
- Each award is attributable to a source/rule version and idempotent; reversals preserve history.
- Raw activity volume alone cannot trivially farm reputation.
- Self-vote/self-reward and duplicate event paths are tested and blocked where policy requires.
- Streak logic is timezone-aware and non-shaming.
- Badges/levels use transparent criteria and do not grant privileged roles automatically.
- Profile UI clearly distinguishes language proficiency from platform points/reputation.
- Stitch/responsive/a11y, tests, abuse controls, commits and CI evidence complete.
